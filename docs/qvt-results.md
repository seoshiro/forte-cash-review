# QVT-01 result: rejected

The official-label-only temporal representation experiment failed its fixed quality gate. TUNE was not run; HOLDOUT remains closed. No manual annotation or external AI answer was read or used. No new model weights were downloaded, no language model was loaded, and no real-video content was emitted to developer tools.

The encoder used the already downloaded Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct visual weights, pinned and SHA256 checked against config/qvt-protocol.json. Its [official weight card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct) declares Apache-2.0; the installed Transformers implementation carries Apache-2.0. Safetensors loaded only the model.visual prefix. This is ordinary visual representation extraction, with no face identification or audio processing.

## Recorded protocol deviation

Registration expected 27 TRAIN manifest rows. The technical-probe ordering excluded all rows sharing a probe SHA, so one exact duplicate negative row was removed. There were 26 unique contents (14 positive, 12 negative), still 24 groups. This bookkeeping error was discovered and recorded during feature extraction, before any quality results were checked, in .private/qvt-control/protocol-deviation-safe-summary.json. The original frozen script/config and raw aggregate result were preserved; no rerun or retrospective selection was performed. Acceptance was independently invalidated and TUNE prohibited even had numerical gates passed. The quality gate failed anyway.

## Technical cost

The registered 1500-second wall budget was respected: 185.157 seconds for the experiment including loading, synthetic checks, extraction and grouped validation. Encoder loading/import/hash verification took 29.719 seconds. Feature extraction including decode averaged 5.8372 seconds per unique content, maximum 15.406 seconds. There were 360 sampled frames, zero technical failures, and all source hashes remained unchanged.

The visual model contained 406,957,056 parameters; language parameters loaded: zero. Torch's observed reserved-memory peak was 868 MiB. Process RSS at the final observation was 2265.84 MiB, not a proven peak. The first three duration-selected TRAIN probes averaged 7.6877 seconds, maximum 15.406 seconds. This is feature-experiment cost, not a public-API latency or 5-minute SLA claim.

## Consequence

The temporal representation is rejected: it is worse than the static comparator and constant-positive F1, has zero specificity and still misses one positive. The static comparator is also rejected. Do not deploy either or start another threshold/seed search on these results.

The remaining blocker is substantive rather than infrastructure: existing measured approaches have not established useful visual detection, and official binary/point labels do not identify event type or prove rule applicability. The user is not being asked to supply missing cash-domain expertise. No automatic rule/type explanation may be manufactured from the binary head. Release still requires useful content-based detection and locally established observation-to-rule conditions, exclusions and evidence relevance. Production credentials and public tunnel remain pending direct authorization; neither was created.
